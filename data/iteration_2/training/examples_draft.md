# Improved training examples — approved draft

Status: approved by the user and converted to [examples.jsonl](examples.jsonl) for iteration 2. Answers were written by the assistant, not generated by the local model. The new dataset has not been used for training. The training script still uses iteration 1's training file.

The new dataset retains the original 12 examples and adds these 30, making 42 examples. This is a small learning experiment, not a guaranteed solution or an exhaustive list of questions.

## Facts and boundaries

Use the same family facts as our [existing answer key](../../iteration_1/evaluation/baseline_questions.md): Dasharatha is the father of Rama, Bharata, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna. Their mothers are Kausalya, Kaikeyi, Sumitra, and Sumitra, respectively. Relationship and counting answers below are deductions from those facts. We are not expanding to other characters or Ramayana traditions.

Keep the existing system instruction for every example:

> Answer the question about the Ramayana in one or two sentences. If you do not know, say "I don't know."

Only the question and proposed answer became user/assistant messages. The headings, IDs, and review notes are not training text.

These additions are informed by the failures we inspected. Different wording does not make overlapping facts or tasks independent: the old tests should now be development checks, not an untouched final exam. Before another training run, prepare separate practice-check questions for choosing a checkpoint and fresh final-test questions that will not guide training. Neither set is drafted here.

## 1. Different wording — 6 additions

Practise retrieving the same facts through several phrasings, including paired parent questions.

### D01
**Question:** Rama is the son of which queen?

**Proposed answer:** Rama is the son of Queen Kausalya.

### D02
**Question:** Whom should I name as Rama's mother?

**Proposed answer:** You should name Kausalya as Rama's mother.

### D03
**Question:** Give both parents of Bharata.

**Proposed answer:** Bharata's father is Dasharatha, and his mother is Kaikeyi.

### D04
**Question:** Who are the father and mother of Lakshmana?

**Proposed answer:** Lakshmana's father is Dasharatha, and his mother is Sumitra.

### D05
**Question:** Complete this family record: Shatrughna — father and mother.

**Proposed answer:** Shatrughna's father is Dasharatha, and his mother is Sumitra.

### D06
**Question:** Name the children of Sumitra in Dasharatha's family.

**Proposed answer:** Sumitra's children are Lakshmana and Shatrughna.

## 2. Relationships — 6 additions

Include both shared and different parentage. Short explanations make the intended relationship explicit.

### D07
**Question:** How is Lakshmana related to Bharata?

**Proposed answer:** Lakshmana and Bharata are brothers who share their father, Dasharatha. Their mothers are Sumitra and Kaikeyi, respectively.

### D08
**Question:** Does Shatrughna have the same father as Rama?

**Proposed answer:** Yes, Shatrughna and Rama share the same father, Dasharatha.

### D09
**Question:** Do Rama and Shatrughna have the same mother?

**Proposed answer:** No, Rama's mother is Kausalya, while Shatrughna's mother is Sumitra.

### D10
**Question:** Are Sumitra's two sons also sons of Dasharatha?

**Proposed answer:** Yes, Lakshmana and Shatrughna are sons of both Sumitra and Dasharatha.

### D11
**Question:** List Bharata's brothers among Dasharatha's four sons.

**Proposed answer:** Bharata's brothers are Rama, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna.

### D12
**Question:** Which of Dasharatha's sons have a different mother from Lakshmana?

**Proposed answer:** Rama and Bharata have different mothers from Lakshmana. Their mothers are Kausalya and Kaikeyi, while Lakshmana's mother is Sumitra.

## 3. Connecting facts — 4 additions

Show a brief factual connection, not just a final name. These examples teach specific connections; success would not by itself prove general reasoning.

### D13
**Question:** Name the father of Kausalya's son.

**Proposed answer:** Kausalya's son is Rama, and Rama's father is Dasharatha.

### D14
**Question:** Who are the brothers of Kaikeyi's son?

**Proposed answer:** Kaikeyi's son is Bharata. His brothers are Rama, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna.

### D15
**Question:** A prince has the same mother as Lakshmana but is not Lakshmana; name him.

**Proposed answer:** The prince is Shatrughna, because Sumitra is the mother of both Lakshmana and Shatrughna.

### D16
**Question:** Who is the father shared by the two sons of Sumitra?

**Proposed answer:** Sumitra's sons are Lakshmana and Shatrughna, and their father is Dasharatha.

## 4. Counting — 4 additions

State the count and the names so the answer can be checked. Counts stay within the four-prince family tree.

### D17
**Question:** How many sons of Dasharatha have Sumitra as their mother?

**Proposed answer:** Two sons have Sumitra as their mother: Lakshmana and Shatrughna.

### D18
**Question:** Among the four princes, how many are sons of Kaikeyi?

**Proposed answer:** One prince is Kaikeyi's son: Bharata.

### D19
**Question:** Excluding Rama, how many of Dasharatha's four sons remain?

**Proposed answer:** Three remain: Bharata, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna.

### D20
**Question:** Among the four princes, how many are not sons of Kaikeyi?

**Proposed answer:** Three are not sons of Kaikeyi: Rama, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna.

## 5. Correcting mistaken assumptions — 6 additions

Correct the mistaken relationship directly and provide the right fact. The valid yes/no questions in section 2 help avoid teaching an automatic "no" response.

### D21
**Question:** Is Sumitra the mother of Rama?

**Proposed answer:** No, Rama's mother is Kausalya. Sumitra is the mother of Lakshmana and Shatrughna.

### D22
**Question:** I wrote that Kausalya is Bharata's mother; is that correct?

**Proposed answer:** No, Bharata's mother is Kaikeyi. Kausalya is Rama's mother.

### D23
**Question:** Why is Lakshmana described as Kaikeyi's son?

**Proposed answer:** That description is incorrect: Lakshmana's mother is Sumitra, not Kaikeyi. Kaikeyi's son is Bharata.

### D24
**Question:** Since Shatrughna is Rama's son, who is Shatrughna's mother?

**Proposed answer:** Shatrughna is Rama's brother, not his son. Shatrughna's mother is Sumitra.

### D25
**Question:** Is Dasharatha the mother of the four princes?

**Proposed answer:** No, Dasharatha is their father. Rama's mother is Kausalya, Bharata's is Kaikeyi, and Lakshmana and Shatrughna's is Sumitra.

### D26
**Question:** List the two sons of Kausalya among Dasharatha's four sons.

**Proposed answer:** Only one of the four is Kausalya's son: Rama. The question incorrectly assumes that she has two sons among them.

## 6. Missing information and a solvable contrast — 4 additions

Distinguish an ambiguous clue, a detail that was never supplied, and a clue that does identify someone. Do not label an ordinary known family fact as unknowable merely because it was absent from training.

### D27
**Question:** A note names a prince whose parents are Dasharatha and Sumitra, but gives no name; can you identify exactly one prince?

**Proposed answer:** No, those parents match both Lakshmana and Shatrughna. Another detail is needed to identify one of them.

### D28
**Question:** Someone mentions a brother of Bharata without any other clues; which brother do they mean?

**Proposed answer:** There is not enough information to choose one. They could mean Rama, Lakshmana, or Shatrughna.

### D29
**Question:** In an invented scene, Bharata carries a bag whose color is never specified; what color is it?

**Proposed answer:** I don't know. The invented scene does not specify the bag's color.

### D30
**Question:** A note says a prince's mother is Kaikeyi; which of Dasharatha's four sons does that identify?

**Proposed answer:** That identifies Bharata, whose mother is Kaikeyi.
